Starforge — Idle Miner: Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Starforge — Idle Miner ("the App," "we," "us") handles information. MM Custom Wiring operates this App.

This policy is kept up to date with what the App actually does. Sections marked not active describe features that exist in the App's design but aren't turned on yet — they'll be updated to reflect reality before those features go live.

Summary

This is a single-player idle game. As of closed testing, the App does not collect or transmit any personal information — your progress is saved only on your own device. The sections below describe both what's true today and what becomes true once ads, in-app purchases, or analytics are switched on.

Information We Collect

Game data (current)

The App saves your progress (ore, upgrades, achievements, zones unlocked, etc.) locally on your device only. This data is never sent to us or to any server — we have no way to see it. It stays on your device unless you uninstall the App or use its in-game "reset progress" option.

Account information

The App does not require or support creating an account. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifier directly.

Advertising — not active during closed testing

Once real advertising is integrated, the App will show ads through a third-party ad network (network not yet chosen — this section will name it and link to its privacy policy before ads go live). Ad networks typically collect device identifiers (such as your device's advertising ID), general device information, and approximate location, and may use this to show more relevant ads. This data is collected and processed by the ad network directly, under its own privacy policy. You can generally limit ad tracking through your device's settings.

In-app purchases — not active during closed testing

Once real in-app purchases are integrated, all payments will be processed directly by Google Play (and, if released on iOS, the Apple App Store) — not by us. We never see or store your payment card details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed, so we can grant the corresponding in-game item.

Analytics / crash reporting — not currently used

If analytics or crash-reporting tools are added later (e.g. to understand crashes or general usage patterns), this section will be updated first, before that feature ships, to name the specific service and what it collects.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

Since game data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time using the in-game "reset progress" option, or by uninstalling the App. Once ads or analytics are active, your device's system settings generally let you reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your information. Once ads, purchases, or analytics are active, data may be shared with those specific third-party services as described above, solely to provide those features — no data is shared beyond that.

Changes to This Policy

This policy will be updated as the App changes — most notably when ads, purchases, or analytics actually go live. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.
