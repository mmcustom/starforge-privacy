Starforge — Idle Miner: Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Starforge — Idle Miner ("Starforge", "the app") is developed by Mike (MM Custom Wiring). This page explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

Game progress

Starforge does not require an account, login, or any personal information to play. Your game progress (ore, upgrades, Star Cores, and so on) is saved locally on your own device only. It is never sent to us or stored on any server we operate. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, that progress is lost — we have no way to recover it, since we never had it.

Advertising

Starforge shows optional rewarded video ads (for example, the "Watch Ad" button) using Google AdMob. To serve and measure these ads, Google AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, general device information, approximate location, and ad interaction data. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. You can review how Google handles this data in Google's Privacy Policy and manage your ad personalization settings through your device's system settings.

In-app purchases

If Starforge offers in-app purchases (such as removing ads or purchasing in-game currency), those purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive, see, or store your payment card details — Google handles that directly. We may use a third-party service (such as RevenueCat) solely to confirm that a purchase was completed, so the app can unlock the corresponding in-game item; this involves purchase receipt data, not your payment information.

Children's privacy

Starforge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising shown in the app is served according to Google AdMob's policies for the audience the app is configured for in Google Play.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not have a server or database that stores personal information about players. The only data collection involved is what's described above under Advertising and In-app purchases, handled directly by Google.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, when new features are added — we'll update this page and revise the date at the top.

Contact
